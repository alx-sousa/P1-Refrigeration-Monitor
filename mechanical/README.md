# Mechanical integration roadmap

The final enclosure is **not yet complete**.

Current Autodesk Fusion work is a preliminary integration study around the Rev B PCB and selected 1.69-inch display.

## Mechanical requirements already influencing electronics

- single accessible external USB-C connector;
- ESP32-C6 antenna clearance;
- display/FPC placement;
- battery volume and cable routing;
- Hall-sensor position relative to door magnet;
- acoustic opening for the buzzer;
- external probe cable exit/strain relief;
- serviceability for assembly and debugging;
- separation of metal/screws from the Hall/RF-sensitive regions.

## Future deliverables

When the enclosure milestone is reached, this directory should contain:

- Fusion design/reference exports;
- enclosure revision notes;
- manufacturing/print orientation notes;
- assembly screenshots;
- final fit-clearance evidence;
- photographs of the printed enclosure with the real PCB/display/battery.

Current Fusion images should always be captioned as **conceptual/preliminary mechanical integration**, not as a fabricated enclosure.
